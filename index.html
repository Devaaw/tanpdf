The tools you reach for every day

One clear workspace for reading, creating, organizing, protecting, and printing documents.

Read and review

Open PDFs with smooth zoom, page navigation, full-text search, and page jump. Highlight, underline, add notes, text boxes, shapes, arrows, or stamps.

Word documents

Open DOC and DOCX files in the built-in viewer, search inside them, switch between reflow and print layouts, and export DOCX files as clean PDFs.

Create and convert

Build PDFs from images, HTML, or a live web page. Export PDF pages as images, or convert DOCX files without an office suite.

Scan and OCR

Capture receipts, notes, and forms with the camera, improve contrast, and create multi-page PDFs. On-device OCR makes scans searchable, and Extract Text saves text as TXT.

Merge and organize

Combine PDFs, split by page range, extract pages, reorder, rotate, or delete pages. Compress large files and repair PDFs that other apps cannot open.

Print Studio

Prepare N-up grids, booklets, saddle-stitch layouts, cards, zines, and cut-and-stack sheets. Control margins, bleed, crop marks, and page order.

Protect and sign

Add passwords and control printing, copying, or editing permissions. Unlock files you own, sign with a drawn signature, fill forms, flatten, watermark, number pages, and edit metadata.

Image tools

Compress, resize, convert, and strip EXIF metadata from images before adding them to a PDF or sharing them.

A faster workflow

Search every tool, pin favorites, reopen recent files, review history, use dark mode, and work in Arabic or other supported languages with RTL layout.

Your documents stay yours.

TAN PDF Editor processes documents on your device. It does not require an account, upload your files to a server, or include analytics SDKs. The Google Play build shows banner ads through Google AdMob and uses on-device ML Kit for OCR. The open-source builds contain no ads and request no network permission.

  • No account, profile, or sign-in.
  • No cloud upload or remote document storage.
  • No analytics SDKs or document tracking.
  • Recent files and settings stay local.
  • Ads appear only in the Google Play build; documents are never shared with advertisers.
Offline where it matters. Reading, annotating, merging, splitting, compressing, converting DOCX, scanning, OCR, signing, and printing run on your device. Converting a live web page to PDF is the exception: the Play build needs an internet connection to load that page.
