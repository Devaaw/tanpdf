A focused toolkit

Everyday PDF operations in one lightweight app.

View and annotate

Read PDFs with zoom, page navigation, highlights, drawings, and notes.

Merge and split

Combine documents, split files, reorder pages, rotate, extract, or delete pages.

Convert and scan

Turn images into PDFs, export pages as images, or scan paper documents with the camera.

Text recognition

Extract text with on-device OCR. The open-source builds use Tesseract.

Protect and sign

Add passwords, watermarks, signatures, and flatten completed documents.

Image utilities

Compress, resize, convert, and remove metadata from images before sharing.

Your documents stay yours.

The app does not collect document contents or require an account. It does not include analytics SDKs. The Google Play build shows banner ads through Google AdMob; the open-source builds contain no ads and request no network permission.

  • Processing happens on your device.
  • No cloud upload or remote document storage.
  • No account or profile required.
  • Recent files and settings are stored locally.
  • Ads appear only in the Google Play build.
