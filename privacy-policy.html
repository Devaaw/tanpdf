Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

TAN PDF Editor processes documents on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your document contents.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the TAN PDF Editor Android application and this website. The app is an independent project and is not operated by a third-party cloud service.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, email address, or phone number to use the app.
  • We do not collect the contents of your PDFs, images, scans, or extracted text.
  • We do not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. On-device processing

Viewing, editing, converting, compressing, scanning, signing, OCR, and password operations run on your device. Files you open remain in the locations you choose, except for temporary working copies created by Android or by the app to complete an operation.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose the document scanning feature. You can deny this permission and still use the rest of the app.
  • Storage and document access: used through Android's Storage Access Framework so you can select files and save results. The app does not scan your storage in the background.
  • Network: the fdroid and opensource builds request no network permission. The playstore build may include network access for Google ML Kit components.

5. Third-party components

The playstore build uses Google ML Kit for on-device OCR and document scanning. ML Kit may download model components through Google Play services and is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. The app does not send your document contents to a server for OCR.

The fdroid and opensource builds use Tesseract for OCR and do not include Google Play Services.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web access data, such as IP address and user agent, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Data stored on your device

The app stores settings, recent-file references, and operation history locally. Temporary files are removed when the app closes or when you clear the app's cache. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this local data.

7. Data sharing

We do not share app data with third parties. If you choose to share a file using Android's share sheet, the app sends that file only to the app or contact you select.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the update date shown above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or requests relating to local app data can be sent to BenCoder57@gmail.com.
