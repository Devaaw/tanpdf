Data Deletion Request

Last updated: October 29, 2026

TAN PDF Editor is designed with privacy in mind. Most data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted directly from the app.

1) Delete data directly from the app (Recommended)

You can delete most app data yourself without sending a request:

  • Clear Cache: Open Settings → Clear Cache to remove temporary files created during PDF processing, scanning, or conversion.
  • Clear App Data: On your Android device, go to Settings → Apps → TAN PDF Editor → Storage → Clear Data. This removes all local settings, history, and cached files.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling TAN PDF Editor removes all app data stored in the app's private storage by default.

2) What data is deleted or kept

  • Deleted locally: Temporary working files, cached thumbnails, scan images, operation history, and app preferences stored in the app's private storage.
  • Not collected by us: We do not have user accounts, servers, or cloud storage for your PDFs or personal files. Your documents remain under your control.
  • Advertising (Play Store version): The Google Play version uses Google AdMob. Advertising data (such as the Advertising ID) is handled by Google. To limit or reset it, use your device's ad settings or Google's tools. See Google Privacy Policy.

3) Request deletion if needed

If you cannot access the device or have a specific deletion request, you can email us at:

BenCoder57@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your device information (optional)
  • A brief description of your request

Since we do not store your documents or personal data on our servers, most requests can be resolved by clearing app data or uninstalling the app as described above.

Retention

Local data remains on your device until you clear cache, clear app data, or uninstall the app. We do not retain copies of your files.
